Just a quick post to say - I've joined Boston Dynamics, working on their Atlas humanoid robot program, as an electrical engineer!

I'm super excited to advance next generation humanoid robots, solve some gnarly technical problems, and work with their awesome team. Everyone is so kind and holds themselves and the team to very rigorous engineering standards. I can tell it'll be a lot of fun.

I've enjoyed showing the existing Atlas videos ([example 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I44_zbEwz_w), [example 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tF4DML7FIWk), [example 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fn3KWM1kuAw), [example 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=29ECwExc-_M)) to my friends and family, and some of them think my new job is making the robot dance :)
